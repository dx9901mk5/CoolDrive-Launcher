# Privacy Policy — DX-Car Launcher (`com.dxcar.launcher`)

**Effective Date**: 2026-11-24  
**Developer**: DX-Car Launcher (`https://github.com/dx9901mk5/DX-Car-Launcher`)  
**Contact**: `122500891+dx9901mk5@users.noreply.github.com`

DX-Car Launcher is a dual-role Android Car Head Unit / AI Box launcher and Smartphone Companion app. We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or upload your personal data to any third-party advertising servers.

## 1. Permissions & How Data Is Used On-Device

- **Installed Applications (`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`)**: Used strictly on-device to display your installed navigation, media, and utility apps in the Car Launcher App Drawer, Recent Dock, and Split-Screen selector.
- **Accessibility Service (`BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE`)**: Used only when explicitly enabled by the user to provide floating Dock navigation (Home, Back, Recents), split-screen window automation, 3D map tilt/zoom single-finger assist, and remote touchpad pointer actions from your paired smartphone. No keystrokes, passwords, or private screen text are logged or transmitted externally.
- **Notification Listener (`BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE`)**: Used on-device to mirror selected messaging notifications (such as LINE, Messenger, SMS) from your paired phone to your car screen over local Bluetooth/Wi-Fi, display media playback controls, and read Google Maps turn-by-turn navigation alerts.
- **Phone & Call Permissions (`READ_PHONE_STATE`, `ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS`, `CALL_PHONE`, `READ_CONTACTS`)**: Used exclusively to display incoming caller ID on the car head unit banner and allow hands-free Answer / Decline actions while driving.
- **Location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`)**: Used on-device to center the built-in map, display live vehicle speed, calculate sunrise/sunset for auto Day/Night mode, and fetch local weather.
- **Bluetooth & Wi-Fi (`BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`, `CHANGE_WIFI_STATE`)**: Used to connect your Smartphone Companion directly to your Car AI Box over local Bluetooth SPP / Wi-Fi Hotspot without external cloud relays.
- **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`)**: Used only when you tap voice navigation or voice reply.
- **Google Play Billing (`BILLING`)**: Used to manage the 7-day full-feature trial and the optional one-time lifetime unlock purchase via Google Play Store.

## 2. Data Storage & Security
All preferences, custom shortcuts, and offline map tiles are stored locally on your device. No personal messages, call logs, or location histories are stored on developer servers.
